## Privacy notice working text
YuviList is operated by [CONFIRM LEGAL OPERATOR AND CONTACT ADDRESS]. Contact support@yuvilist.com for privacy or support questions. We process account and authentication information, submitted listing and research records, browser preferences, usage/security logs and support communications to provide the private beta, protect accounts, diagnose issues and respond to requests. Optional AI and search requests use selected authorised inputs; do not include buyer data, unnecessary personal information or credentials. Proposed bases for assessment: contract necessity for agreed core service, legitimate interests for proportionate security/support (document balancing assessment), legal obligation where applicable, and a separately assessed lawful basis for optional processing. Explicit per-request UI permission is not automatically legal consent. Confirm before publication.

Current systems: Supabase authentication/database; Cloudflare hosting, Access and mail routing; Resend transactional email and support inbox; Gmail manual fallback/historic correspondence; OpenAI optional AI; DataForSEO optional Google demand; Stripe test-only billing. Verify actual contractual roles, data categories, locations, subprocessors, transfers and safeguards for each, rather than claiming all processing occurs in Ireland. Confirm cookie/browser storage necessity and any consent requirements. No advertising or unrelated reuse proposed. Do not promise provider deletion or retention periods not verified.

Proposed retention: active workspace while necessary to provide service, inactive accounts reviewed after 90 days, support cases reviewed at 12 months, backups and provider logs according to verified retention schedules; exceptions for necessary legal/security records. Confirm actual schedule and lawful bases. Users may request eligible access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent where applicable. Contact support; identity checks will be proportionate. Complaints may be raised with ICO https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/. Required account information is needed to provide account service; optional research and AI can be skipped. No automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect intended; verify actual processing.

## Free beta terms working text
Invitation-only free testing; no obligation to subscribe. Use your own authorised product information, comply with marketplace rules, keep credentials private and verify outputs before use. Scores are internal quality indicators, not marketplace rankings or sales guarantees. No automatic publication to a marketplace. Features may change or be unavailable during testing; notify testers of material changes where practicable. Users may leave at any time and request data export or account closure via support. Retain applicable statutory rights; no blanket liability waiver. Confirm legal operator, scope of service, account eligibility, fair suspension procedure, IP rights, governing law and applicable consumer/business classification before publication. Paid plans need separate pricing, contract, renewal, cancellation, refund, tax and statutory-rights review before checkout.
