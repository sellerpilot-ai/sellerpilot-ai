Start with your shop

1. Add or import your catalogue
  1. Existing testers sign in and open Products.
  2. Add a listing manually, or import a compatible CSV and review the preview before confirming. GBP and USD are supported; no conversion is performed.
  3. Check title, tags, description and verified facts. Imported records are private drafts, not marketplace updates.

Only import information you own or are expressly permitted to use. Direct marketplace imports need provider approval.

2. Review your priority listings
  1. Open Home and find Today's shop assistant.
  2. Filter critical findings or sort by score. Select Review action plan.
  3. Review each recommendation and check factual accuracy. Apply reversible safe fixes when available, or edit the appropriate field.
  4. Analyse again, compare the score, and explicitly save the product or audit snapshot when ready.

Quality scores reflect internal checks, not Etsy ranking, demand, revenue or a promise of sales.

3. Measure your progress
  1. Open Reports and save a weekly health snapshot.
  2. Return in later weeks to compare catalogue quality. A changing catalogue can affect the average.
  3. Review your open tasks and export reports if needed.

Optional weekly snapshots run on a successful workspace sync, not while your browser is closed.

Research & optimisation

4. Discover keywords
  1. Choose a listing or enter a focus phrase and confirmed modifiers in Keyword Lab.
  2. Confirm permission to use the information and select Discover keywords.
  3. Sort, filter and inspect title, description and exact tag coverage. Review tag length and suitability.
  4. Save useful ideas to the device shortlist or private research library, or export the filtered results.

Local relevance is not measured search volume, buyer demand or competition.

5. Compare a competitor observation
  1. Open the Research Hub or use Compare in the assistant.
  2. Choose your listing and enter a dated, independently supplied or expressly authorised observation. Only include competitor tags if authorised.
  3. Compare prices in the same currency, and review exact tag gaps and confirmed differentiators.
  4. Save an observation to your private notebook or download the comparison if useful.

No live fetch, competitor sales estimate or automatic copying is performed. One observation cannot establish a market average.

6. Try AI and profit tools

AI Studio and Image Intelligence are separately gated. Check availability, review consent, and verify generated claims before saving. Never send personal buyer information, private addresses or unauthorised photos. Profit Planner uses your own prices and costs; include fees, postage and other costs as appropriate.

Account, data & billing

Can I register or subscribe?

No public registration or real subscription payments are enabled. Read how to join the beta. Existing testers can use the private workspace. The beta does not require a card. Commercial plan prices and allowances are not final.

Where are my records stored?

Products, audit history and the research notebook sync to the private cloud account. Editorial preferences and the keyword shortlist are account-separated on this device. Sample shop changes are temporary in memory and do not touch your account. See pre-launch data information.

How do I export or request deletion?

In Settings, download workspace data or prepare a privacy request. These files are created on your device. A privacy request is not automatically submitted, and self-service account deletion is not enabled. Keep exported private business data secure.

Support & troubleshooting

The workspace is not loading or syncing

Check the status message, connection and sign-in state. Refresh the page and retry a catalogue sync. If the issue persists, prepare feedback with the affected screen, steps and error message. Do not include passwords, keys, authentication codes or customer information.

How can I send feedback?

Existing testers can open Settings → Help & private beta → Prepare feedback. Download the text file and share it using your established private testing contact. It is not sent automatically. A verified public support contact is still required before commercial launch.

This is a private beta. Live marketplace metrics, public paid AI and production subscriptions are not available. SellerPilot is independent of Etsy.
