PRE-LAUNCH INFORMATION

Data and privacy

Not a final privacy notice. YuviList is in private development. Incoming support email forwarding has been tested at support@yuvilist.com. The legal operator, formal rights-request procedure, retention schedule, international transfer safeguards and final lawful bases are still to be confirmed. The public beta preparation checklist does not collect or transmit applicant data. Public onboarding must not be promoted until the complete notice has been approved.

Sample shop

The sample shop uses fictional product records and runs in the browser. Its edits are not written to the private catalogue and reset on reload. The website host may process normal network requests and operational logs.

Existing test accounts

The workspace uses Supabase authentication and cloud storage for account information, product listings, saved audit history and workspace records. Account-scoped database policies are designed to restrict access to the user's own data. Some display and research preferences are stored in the browser.

Optional AI and marketplace services

Text generation is available to an authorised private beta account and sends selected product facts to OpenAI after explicit confirmation. Photo analysis remains separately gated. When enabled, a prepared image is sent only after confirmation. Do not send Etsy-sourced data to AI without Etsy's explicit permission. Marketplace retrieval requires an authorised provider connection. Do not enter buyer addresses, private documents or unnecessary personal information.

Your information

Existing testers can export their currently synced catalogue, audit history, research workspace and account-scoped editorial preferences and shortlist. Authentication logs, provider records and unsaved drafts are not included. Testers may download a privacy-request draft and send it to support@yuvilist.com; the file is not submitted automatically. Please do not attach passwords, recovery links or API keys. We will verify identity proportionately before disclosing or deleting personal data. Product deletion is available in the workspace. Full account deletion, retention and verified rights-request procedures remain launch-readiness items.

Data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law provides rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis. You can contact support about these rights or complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. We aim to respond without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines. The complete operator and retention details remain subject to approval.

Before inviting new testers

We will confirm the data controller, contact information, purposes and lawful bases, recipients, retention, transfers, rights and complaint procedure before making the final privacy notice available.
