PRE-LAUNCH INFORMATION

Data and privacy

Not a final privacy notice. SellerPilot is in private development. The legal operator, support address, retention schedule, international transfer safeguards and final lawful bases are still to be confirmed. Public onboarding must not be promoted until the complete notice has been approved.

Sample shop

The sample shop uses fictional product records and runs in the browser. Its edits are not written to the private catalogue and reset on reload. The website host may process normal network requests and operational logs.

Existing test accounts

The workspace uses Supabase authentication and cloud storage for account information, product listings, saved audit history and workspace records. Account-scoped database policies are designed to restrict access to the user's own data. Some display and research preferences are stored in the browser.

Optional AI and marketplace services

AI generation and photo analysis are gated pending approval. When activated, the selected product information or prepared photo will be sent to the AI provider only after a user confirms the request. Marketplace retrieval requires an authorised provider connection. Do not enter buyer addresses, private documents or unnecessary personal information.

Your information

Existing testers can export their product catalogue using workspace settings. Product deletion is available in the workspace; full account deletion, retention and formal rights-request procedures are launch-readiness items.

Before public launch

We will confirm the data controller, contact information, purposes and lawful bases, recipients, retention, transfers, rights and complaint procedure before making the final privacy notice available.
