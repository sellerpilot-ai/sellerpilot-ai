COMMON QUESTIONS

What should I know?

Do I need an Etsy connection?

No. The demo, private catalogue and compatible CSV import can be used independently of live marketplace access. GBP and USD prices are supported for CSV import. No currency conversion is performed.

Does a higher listing score guarantee sales?

No. Our score measures internal content and completeness checks. It does not measure Etsy's ranking algorithm, search demand or future sales.

Does SellerPilot automatically change my Etsy shop?

No. Current edits are private drafts. External changes would require appropriate marketplace permissions and explicit seller approval.

Can I use AI generation now?

AI text generation has been tested with a restricted private beta account. Photo analysis remains separately gated. Public AI access and paid subscriptions are not available.

Can I subscribe today?

No. This is a pre-launch preview, and no paid subscriptions are offered yet.

How can I contact support?

See the beta access information. A verified business support channel is not yet available; existing testers can use their established private testing contact.
